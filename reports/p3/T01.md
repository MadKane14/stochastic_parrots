## What I learned
- A 4-core / 30 GiB Kaggle CPU kernel runs `check`, the tests, both smoke runs and a full `val_v1@0.1` dev run (1116 s; see T05) without errors. `summary.txt` shows every stage `rc=0`.
- There's **no swap and a hard 30 GiB cgroup limit**. T05's full-density blocking peaked at 30.0 GB for `p1_compound`, right at the limit. Anything bigger (`--qfrac 1`, ~37–40 GB per TASKS/p1's 20:51 run) will be OOM-killed here and needs phoenix.
