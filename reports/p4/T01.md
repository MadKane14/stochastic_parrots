## Surprises / bugs
- **Kaggle image conflict:** installing the pinned `torch==2.8.0` over the image's torch 2.10 leaves the image's
  `torchvision 0.25` broken (`RuntimeError: operator torchvision::nms does not exist`), and `transformers` then fails
  to import `BertModel`. Fix in the runner: `pip uninstall -y torchvision torchaudio` (the pipeline uses neither).
  Anyone else on Kaggle/Colab with `requirements-gpu.txt` will hit this.
- Full `val_v1` and the full-density `blk_eval.py` (~30–37 GB) will not fit in Kaggle's 31 GB. p4 can run `@0.1`
  splits and GPU jobs only.
