## What changed since T06
- `p4_e5` rewritten to be memory-lean for full-train pair sets:
  - cosines are computed in chunks straight from cache rows, with no per-id matrix;
  - the cache is loaded once with no sorted copy of the vectors;
  - `BER_EMB_READONLY` lets it use a precomputed cache;
  - numpy rank/gap is computed within each S1 **and** within each S2/S3 record (`group_sides`), following p1's
    `_group` pattern.
  Column names are backward compatible with T06. A synthetic test (brute-force cosines and pandas rank/gap
  reference) passes.
- `configs/exp/p4/T12_e5.yaml` = `inherit: ../p1/T08_sub.yaml` + `p4_e5` on fields `[name, full]` (the fields the
  planner asked for) with group features on both sides. It produces 10 columns: 2 cosines + (gap, rank) × 2 fields
  × 2 sides.
- `exploration/p4/e5_embed_all.py`: precomputes the whole cache (one field per GPU in parallel) and reports
  unique-text counts, time and texts/s.
