### Points that could be read as a rule issue (CLAUDE.md §2) — for the lead
1. **All transductive rows** rest on open question Q7 (fit unsupervised statistics on test), provisionally accepted (D6).
   They use no labels; state it plainly in the methodology.
2. **`p1_selftrain` is the one real risk**: pseudo-labels on test. Our conservative reading of Q7 was "unsupervised
   stats yes, **pseudo-labels no**". If it is used, say so explicitly; if the #9 gain is small, the safer final is T18.
3. Generic word lists in the normalizer config (`legal_tokens: [inc, llc, ltd, pvt, ...]`) are a hand-written but
   country-agnostic list (Q6 reading: generic lists yes). No country-value branches: `idf_group: country` groups by
   equal strings, never tests a value.
4. `p4_tcanon`'s abbreviation lists are learned, not written; its unit tests (`exploration/p4/test_unsup_canon.py`)
   use French-looking synthetic tokens only as test data.
